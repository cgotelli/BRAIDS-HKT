## Boundary contract confirmed by the user

- The computation domain is a polygon. Every unassigned edge or edge interval is a wall; an outlet is never inferred.
- Users select exactly one polygon face for each inlet and one or several faces for an outlet or explicit wall. Several different inlet and outlet IDs may be active simultaneously, including several boundaries of the same type. Unassigned and deleted faces are walls.
- A logical boundary (`bc_id`) may own multiple selected edge segments. Geometry IDs remain separate from hydraulic IDs. Disconnected sections need an explicit allocation/control rule; they must not silently become one connected cross-section.
- Inlet discharge is the **total for its boundary ID**, distributed over its mapped openings once. Selecting another edge must not duplicate that discharge. Integrate accepted water, suspended and bedload supply/export over the same faces and record both per-ID and domain totals.
- Boundary sections use the represented terrain, horizontal water elevation, and filling from the connected lowest basin. Inward normals, partial openings, geometry changes and bed evolution must remain consistent with the solver fluxes.
- Outlet water level, weir/critical control or supercritical outflow must be explicitly chosen. Reversal/backflow and incoming sediment during reversal need explicit policies.
- QGIS must persist the polygon, selections, IDs and settings, preview the actual solver openings, and reject overlapping assignments and stale geometry IDs.

The [polygon data model](../braids_core/boundaries/domain.py) supports multiple assignments and shared logical IDs. The [native cut-cell path](../braids_core/boundaries/cut_cells.py) maps arbitrary polygon edges to solver faces. The first [BRAIDS QGIS vertical slice](../validation/qgis/vertical_slice/reports/QGIS_VERTICAL_SLICE.md) persists those selections, executes the actual resource reservoir and loads its results. The scheduled example now also plots requested and accepted per-ID boundary histories. The remaining items below concern additional spatial inputs, release packaging and broader qualification.
